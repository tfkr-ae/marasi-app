# Privacy policy

Marasi App (the desktop app and the iOS and Android apps) collects no data about you.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.
- **The app talks only to the Marasi service you connect it to**, on your own computer or server. The traffic, findings and other project data you see live in that service, under your control.
- **Saved connections stay on your device.** A connection's password is stored in the device's keychain (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore, or the desktop's credential store).
- **The camera** is used only to scan a pairing QR code, on your device. No image leaves it.
- **Location and Face ID** are never requested. The framework the app is built on references them; the app doesn't use them.
- **Updates (desktop only)** are checked against this project's GitHub releases.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/tfkr-ae/marasi-app/issues).

Last updated: 13 November 2026.
