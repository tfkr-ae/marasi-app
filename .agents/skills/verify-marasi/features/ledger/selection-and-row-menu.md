# Ledger: selection, the row menu and bulk changes

## Driving it with Chrome CDP

**Selection and the row menu.** Pressing a row selects it alone; dragging across rows sweeps them; `⌘`-click toggles, `⇧`-click selects from the anchor, `⌘⇧`-click adds that range. Drive them with `"modifiers"` on a click (`{"click":".ledger-row","hasText":"/b","modifiers":["shift"]}`, `["cmd"]`) and the sweep with a `drag` step (`{"drag":".ledger-row","hasText":"/a","to":".ledger-row","toHasText":"/c"}`). With the table focused, `⇧↓`/`⇧↑` extend from the anchor (the driver sends `shift+down` and `shift+up`), `cmd+a` selects the whole query or live view as a Marasi selection (query, exclusion and the results' as_of, never ids; every loaded row up to that as_of shows selected, later new matches do not), and `escape` clears. Selected rows carry `aria-selected="true"`; the focused one, which the drawer shows, is outlined. The status bar reads `Request N selected`, `N selected`, or after `cmd+a` `All N matching selected`, plus ` · M new matches not included` once new matches arrive (`#ledgerSelectionCount`). Right-click a row (`{"click":".ledger-row","hasText":"<path>","button":"right"}`; on a selected row the menu acts on the selection, on any other row on that row alone, and either way the selection, the focus and the drawer stay as they were, so a closed drawer stays closed) or press `cmd+shift+.` (or, with the table focused, the ContextMenu key or `⇧F10`: `{"key":"contextmenu"}`, `{"key":"shift+f10"}`, which open it for the focused row; `LedgerTable.svelte` `menukey`) to open `.ledger-row-menu`: a header summing up what it acts on, a `Type an action…` filter (`.ledger-row-menu-filter`, which searches submenus too), then items `[data-menu-item=<id>]` (`websocket`, `launchpad`, `armory`, `browser-view`, `finding`, `testcase`, `note`, `highlight`, `copy`, `narrow`, `select`, `metadata`, `delete`). Submenus nest, one group each, with no headings: `launchpad` holds `lp-new`, `lp-each` (several pairs), `lp-add` (Add to launchpad ›, the launchpads as `lp-<id>`; disabled `No launchpads yet` without any) and `lp-remove` (Remove from launchpad ›, `lpu-<id>`); `finding` and `testcase` hold `f-new`/`t-new`, `f-link`/`t-link` (Link to … ›, `f-<id>` with check states) and, when something is linked, `f-unlink`/`t-unlink` (`fu-<id>`/`tu-<id>`); `narrow` ends with `q-hide` (Hide type ›, `x-<type>`). Hover each level in turn (`{"hover":"[data-menu-item=launchpad]"},{"hover":"[data-menu-item=lp-add]"},{"click":"[data-menu-item=lp-<id>]"}`). With several pairs the labels name the count: `3 URLs`, `3 requests as cURL commands`, `3 raw requests`, `3 requests as a Markdown table`, `3 request numbers`. With several pairs, `note` is a submenu: `n-append` (`Append a note…`, a prompt modal) and `n-clear` (`Clear notes…`). `delete` (`Delete from project…` / `Delete N from project…`) and `n-clear` open the confirm modal naming the count (Delete warns `… evidence in a finding.` when any pair is); nothing changes until it is confirmed. After `cmd+a` over more rows than are loaded, `armory`, `copy`, `lp-each`, `f-new` and `t-new` are disabled (`Not for all matching`), their shortcuts (`⌘⇧A`, `⌘⇧F`, `⌘⇧T`, the copy keys) only toast `Not for all matching`, and the header reads `All N matching` without the host, method and status breakdown. `select` lists `s-highlight-<n>` for each highlight among the selected pairs (`All with this highlight`, or `All with highlight <label>` when there are several). Items that would change nothing for the loaded selection are disabled with their reason as the hint (a swatch every selected pair already has: `Already highlighted`; `h-none` with nothing highlighted: `Nothing to clear`; `n-clear` with no notes: `No notes to clear`; a finding or test case every pair is linked to: `Already linked`); a disabled swatch has `aria-disabled="true"` and its reason shows at the row's end on hover. Under a partly loaded ⌘A selection nothing is disabled for this. Marasi refuses to delete a pair whose Launchpad launch is still pending, or to remove it from the launchpad it was launched from (marasi `docs/adr/2026-10-18-a-pending-launchs-pair-stays-in-its-launchpad.md`), so for such a pair `delete` and that launchpad's `lpu-<id>` are disabled with `N launching` (other launchpads' `lpu-<id>` stay enabled). A ⌘A selection whose pairs are all loaded is greyed the same way; a partly loaded one stays enabled and, on Marasi's `launch_pending` refusal, toasts `N pairs are still launching; nothing was deleted` (or `…removed`) and selects the refused rows. Proven with a launch kept pending by a slow origin (see `../launchpad/send-launch-compare.md`): `{"click":".ledger-row","hasText":"/slow?sleep=600","button":"right"},{"hover":"[data-menu-item=launchpad]"},{"hover":"[data-menu-item=lp-remove]"}`, then read `title="1 launching"` on `[data-menu-item=delete]` and the launched-from `lpu-<id>`. Bulk changes run once on the selection and toast Marasi's count, and say nothing when Marasi changed no pair of a listed selection (removing from a launchpad they aren't in, say); after ⌘A, whose pairs aren't all loaded, they say why instead (`None of the 1,200 matching requests had a highlight`, `All 3 matching requests were already in "Login"`, `The matching request had no note`) (`Added 3 requests to "…"`, `Removed 1 request from "…"`, `Appended a note to 2 requests`, `Cleared 2 notes`, `Deleted 2 requests`); the rows are read again in place. The `highlight` item is a row of swatches (`.ledger-highlight-swatches`, buttons `[data-menu-item=h-1]` to `h-8` and `h-none`, `role=menuitemradio`, `aria-checked` `true`/`mixed`/`false`); hovering one shows its label at the row's end, and with the row active `left`/`right` pick a swatch and `enter` applies it. Typing a label in the filter lists the swatch as `Highlight › <label>`. Applying one toasts Marasi's count (`Highlighted 2 requests "Red"`, `Cleared the highlight on 1 request`) and repaints the rows' 3px stripe and tint in place. Submenus open in `.ledger-row-submenu[data-menu-level="<n>"]`, flush against their parent with their first item level with the parent item, running up instead (last item level) when they don't fit below. A first-level submenu opens on the side with room for every level below it, and deeper levels keep that side; the menu itself opens above the pointer near the bottom and left of it near the right edge. The placement rules are unit-tested in `menuPlacement.test.js`. Keys: arrows move, `arrowright`/`enter` open a submenu, `arrowleft` goes back, `enter` runs, `escape` closes the deepest submenu first (so a reset may need two). The menu opens with no item active, so send `down` before `right` (`right` with nothing active does nothing). Proven: `{"click":".ledger-row","hasText":"/b.html","button":"right"},{"key":"down"},{"key":"right"},{"waitSelector":".ledger-row-submenu[data-menu-level=\"1\"]"},{"key":"left"},{"waitNoSelector":".ledger-row-submenu"},{"key":"escape"}`. The pointer opens the menu with a real right-click (a CDP `mousePressed`/`mouseReleased` pair with `button: "right"`, which Chrome turns into `contextmenu`); `enter` is its own feature (run the active item or open its submenu), not a stand-in for it. Each change reports through a toast (`Linked 2 requests to finding "…"` with the count Marasi changed, `Selected N requests`, `Narrowed the query: …`, `Copied 2 URLs`). `browser-view` (`Copy browser view URL`, also on the drawer's rail and column and `⌘⇧V` with the drawer open, which yields to a focused editor) copies `http://marasi/view/<uuid>` and toasts `Copied the browser view URL. Open it in a browser that uses Marasi's proxy`; it is disabled for several pairs (`One request at a time`), a pair without a response (`No response yet`) and a WebSocket upgrade (`Not for a WebSocket upgrade`). What the URL serves is in `../launchpad/menus.md`. Proven: `{"click":".ledger-row","hasText":"/compass-allowed.html","button":"right"},{"click":"[data-menu-item=browser-view]"},{"waitText":"browser view URL","within":".snackbar-wrapper"}`, and with the drawer open on a row `{"key":"cmd+shift+v"}` with the same toast. Shortcuts with a selection: `⌘⇧L` new launchpad, `⌘⇧A` Armory, `⌘⇧F` new finding, `⌘⇧T` new test case, `⌘⇧N` note, `⌘⇧C` copy URLs, `⌘⇧M` metadata, `⌘⇧.` the menu (`⌘.` stays Jump to Toast and `⌘L` Setup Listener).
